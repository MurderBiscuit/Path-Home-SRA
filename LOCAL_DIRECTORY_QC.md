## Special restrictions
- The **Clerk** is not an all-purpose state-ID agency or correct birth office for a person born elsewhere.
- **Love INC** specifically says it does NOT cover hotel/motel, rent/mortgage/security deposits, gas cards or phone bills; the app must not suggest those services from this listing.
- LSSCM is **civil** legal aid; no implied criminal defense representation.
- Housing support is eligibility/funding dependent; HARA handles assessment and routing, not guaranteed placement.
- JATA scheduling depends on service and destination; confirm same-day capacity and transit hours before the trip.
- None of these agencies is necessarily the person's assigned parole/probation reporting office.
