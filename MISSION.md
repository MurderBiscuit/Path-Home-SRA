# PATH HOME

## Structural Reentry Assistance

*Because freedom deserves a fighting chance.*

## Our Mission

Every year, hundreds of thousands of men and women leave correctional institutions and return to a world that has continued moving without them. They step beyond prison walls only to encounter new barriers: uncertainty, financial hardship, complicated legal obligations, and a society that often expects them to succeed without providing the tools to do so. They are expected to find housing, secure employment, rebuild relationships, and reclaim their independence, often with little guidance or support. For many, the greatest obstacle is not a lack of determination, but the absence of a clear path forward.

Path Home: Structural Reentry Assistance was founded on the belief that freedom should mean more than simply opening a prison gate. True freedom requires knowledge, opportunity, dignity, and the ability to make informed decisions about one's future. Our mission is to ensure that individuals returning to society have access to the legal information, practical resources, personalized guidance, and community connections necessary to rebuild their lives with confidence and purpose.

We believe that justice should never be determined by wealth, that understanding one's rights should never be a privilege, and that rehabilitation deserves more than a promise of a second chance. Behind every conviction is a human being: a parent hoping to reunite with their children, a loved one seeking forgiveness, or an individual determined to become more than the circumstances of their past. We recognize that accountability and compassion can coexist, and that meaningful change becomes possible when people are given the support and opportunity to achieve it.

Our vision reaches beyond creating an application. We seek to bridge the divide between incarceration and independence, remove unnecessary barriers to successful reentry, reduce preventable violations caused by confusion, and strengthen the families and communities that welcome returning citizens home. Through accessible technology, reliable legal information, and compassionate guidance, Path Home will work to transform one of life's most difficult transitions into a journey defined by preparation, stability, and hope.

We believe that a person's worst chapter should never be allowed to write the ending of their story. A prison sentence may take away years of someone's life, but it should never extinguish their opportunity to build a meaningful future. When we help individuals reclaim their independence, we do more than change individual lives. We reunite families, strengthen communities, restore dignity, and reaffirm our shared humanity. Because coming home should not mean facing the world alone, and every person deserves the opportunity to move forward.

**Everyone deserves a Path Home.**
