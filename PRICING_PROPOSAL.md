# Path Home pricing recommendation (proposal, not implemented)

**Recommended: free core access for returning individuals and families.** The value proposition is reducing avoidable legal mistakes and increasing stability; a paywall at download would exclude some people who need the app most.

1. Free individual app: case-law links, source-backed legal guidance, reminders, support resources, money and ID planning, local-first private profile.
2. Optional $1.99 one-time supporter purchase: a voluntary way to help fund maintenance. No paywall for core legal information, reminders, or safety.
3. Organization subscriptions: license program-level onboarding material, curated state/jurisdiction research updates, educator/trainer deployment, and training materials. No access to identifiable user profiles. Price after validation of willingness-to-pay rather than guessing revenue.
4. Grant, public defense, library and nonprofit sponsorship to subsidize content vetting, accessibility and ongoing legal updates.

App store policy is dynamic. Apple Small Business Program currently advertises a 15% reduced rate for eligible purchases; Google Play fee structure depends on product and eligibility (see official help pages). We should register developer accounts, implement appropriate payments only after finalizing the business model, and never imply an APK or App Store approval exists today.
